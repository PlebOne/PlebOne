Projects

Open-source Nostr tools

Everything here is open source. Each project lives in its own repository, where you can read the code, file issues, and send changes.

  • relay.pleb.one

    A public Nostr relay with whitelist-based access. A Rust relay server does the work, and a Next.js site handles whitelist management and invites. Runs with Docker Compose.

    PlebOne/relay.pleb.one

  • Pleb Signer

    A NIP-55 compatible Nostr signer for Linux, similar to Amber for Android. It runs in the system tray, keeps your keys encrypted at rest, and lets other apps ask for signatures over D-Bus, with your approval.

    PlebOne/Pleb_Signer

  • Noscli

    A command-line Nostr client built with Go and Charm tools (Bubble Tea, Lip Gloss). Sign in with Pleb Signer or an nsec, then read your feed, post, reply, follow threads, and send encrypted DMs from the terminal.

    PlebOne/Noscli
