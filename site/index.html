PlebOne

A small home for open-source Nostr tools

PlebOne builds open-source tools for the Nostr protocol and runs a community relay.

We build tools that respect user privacy, resist censorship, and promote open standards. We're not here to chase hype, just to build solid, useful tools. Everything we make is on GitHub.

The relay

relay.pleb.one

A community Nostr relay, run in the open. Only members on the relay's whitelist can publish to it, and invites are reviewed by people, not bots.

Relay address: wss://relay.pleb.one

How to connect

  1. Request an invite at relay.pleb.one/request. Share your npub and a short note about how you use Nostr.
  2. Someone from the admin team reviews it and replies by Nostr DM.
  3. Once you're in, add wss://relay.pleb.one to the relay list in your Nostr client.

Members can sign in at relay.pleb.one and invite friends from their dashboard. The relay's code is open source at PlebOne/relay.pleb.one.
